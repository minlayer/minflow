# Trademark policy

The source code in this repository is released under its stated open source
licence. **The name and logo are not covered by that licence.** This is the
same split used by the Linux Foundation, the Rust Foundation, and the Python
Software Foundation: permissive code, reserved marks. It exists so that a
broken or malicious fork cannot ship under our identity.

## The marks

This policy covers:

- the word marks **minlayer**, **minflow**, and **minlayer-claude**
- the offset-square device, in every colour variant and file format, as
  specified in [`BRAND.md`](https://github.com/minlayer/.github/blob/main/brand/BRAND.md)

Below, "the mark" means any one of these, and "this project" means the
minlayer project as a whole.

## You may, without asking

- Use the mark to refer to this project — in articles, talks, comparisons,
  documentation, and course material.
- State that your software is "built on", "compatible with", or "powered by"
  this project, in plain text, where that is true.
- Reproduce the mark unmodified, at any size, with its `2u` clearspace intact,
  when linking to this project.
- Redistribute the project unmodified, mark included.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use the mark, or anything confusingly similar, as the logo or icon of your
  own product, service, or fork.
- Modify the mark — recolour, rotate, add effects, place it in a container,
  alter its ratios, or combine it with other marks.
- Use the mark in a way that implies endorsement, affiliation, or official
  status you do not have.
- Register the mark, or a similar mark, in any jurisdiction.
- Use the mark on merchandise offered for sale.

## Forks

You are free to fork. If you distribute a modified version, replace the name
and the mark with your own. You may still say what you forked from — "a fork
of X" is accurate, useful, and welcome.
